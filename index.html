Service details

What to expect from Spectrum

Every service is delivered with safety, compliance, and clear communication from quote to completion.

Electrical Installations (Domestic & Industrial)

We provide expert electrical installations for homes and industrial facilities. From new wiring to panel upgrades, our certified electricians ensure your systems are safe, efficient, and compliant.

Certificate of Compliance (COCs)

Need to sell or lease your property? We issue COCs for electrical work, ensuring your property meets the South African National Standards for safety and legal compliance.

House Renovations

Transform your living space with our renovation services. We handle everything from layout redesign to finishing touches with quality craftsmanship and attention to detail.

Floor Tiling, Plumbing, Painting & Paving

We enhance the appearance and functionality of your home with expert tiling, plumbing repairs, painting services, and durable paving solutions for driveways and outdoor areas.

Solar Installations

Power your home or business sustainably with our reliable solar panel installations. We offer full solutions including system design, installation, and maintenance.

Construction Services

From foundations to roofing, we manage construction projects of all sizes. Our team delivers high-quality, cost-effective construction solutions on time and on budget.

Electric Fencing & Automatic Gates

Secure your property with state-of-the-art electric fencing and automated gate systems. We install, maintain, and repair all types of perimeter security solutions.

Mechanical Repairs (FMCG Industries)

We specialize in mechanical repairs for the Fast-Moving Consumer Goods industry, ensuring your machinery stays operational and efficient with minimal downtime.

Assistance with Shutdowns

Our team supports planned industrial shutdowns, providing skilled labor and technical services to ensure your operations resume quickly and safely.

Rubble Removal

Keep your site clean and safe with our rubble and debris removal services. Perfect for post-renovation, construction, and yard clean-up needs.

Clear View Fence Installations

We install clear view fences for maximum visibility and security, ideal for residential, commercial, and industrial properties.
